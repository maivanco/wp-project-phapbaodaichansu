# Design & Typography Rules

## Font Family & Inheritance Rules

1. **No Secondary Font Classes**:
   - Do NOT use secondary font utility classes such as `font-serif`, `font-mono`, or custom font family rules in templates, components, or partials.
2. **Inherit Font from Body**:
   - All sections, headings (`h1` through `h6`), paragraphs, badges, buttons, cards, and interactive elements must inherit their font family directly from the `body` tag.
3. **Typography Consistency**:
   - Maintain uniform font family styling throughout all landing pages, WooCommerce templates, and theme partials to ensure visual cohesion across the entire site.
